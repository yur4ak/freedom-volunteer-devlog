FreedomVolunteer

A volunteer pilot, a battered Kestrel fighter and a frontier at war. Fly out of Kyiv Prime for the Coalition — Ukraine, with the EU and the US behind it — against the fictional CRINK pact. Trade and build factories, take contracts, defend the front from raids, liberate occupied sectors, then bring down the Zorus Citadel.

Prototype complete · v9 Preparing public release Free to play

What's in it

33 sectors, one economy

Jump gates link hand-placed sectors. Eleven wares flow between stations that produce and consume them; prices follow stock.

A front that moves

Raids, occupation, liberation and counter-offensives, driven by pact strength and coalition morale.

Instruments you earn

The stock navigation suite lags, flickers and loses lock. Better suites, reactors and software sharpen the picture.

Enemies with character

Dodging kamikaze drones, gun-run fighters, snipers, minelayers, carriers, EW jammers, flagships — and ranks that rise with yours.

Solid voxel asteroids

Carve giant rocks one cube at a time, then scan and map the hidden veins inside.

Your own operation

Wingmen, hired trader pilots, missions, factories, a pilot log and a campaign save.

Bonus singularities

Black holes lead to an event-horizon tunnel and a tesseract that wraps space around you.

Made in code

Every ship, station and rock is generated as voxels; all sound and music are synthesized live.

Play

Coming soon on itch.io — free, in a desktop browser, with mouse and keyboard. An optional donation of $1 or more will unlock small cosmetic supporter perks. Nothing that affects combat or the economy is for sale.

  2. 13 Oct 2026Release prep

    Rights, credits and security

    • The game stays all rights reserved; its source is private
    • A credits screen will carry the open-source licenses the game relies on
    • Supporter codes will be checked by a small server and returned as signed tokens, so no secret ships inside the game
  3. 11 Oct 2026Release prep

    The publishing plan

    • itch.io chosen: it handles payments, tax and refunds, and web games are first-class there
    • Perks will be cosmetic only: liveries, a supporter badge, a Volunteer Wall, extra save slots, HUD themes
  4. v9Latest build

    Jammers, warrior ranks and a sound fix

    • Zhitel EW jammers: slow, shielded ships whose 500 m fields scramble your sensors; three overlapping fields blind you
    • Enemy warrior ranks from Ryadovoy to General, scaled to your own pilot level — tougher pilots, better loot
    • Factions pay high-ranked pilots to patrol; salvaging abandoned cargo earns rank
    • Rank ladders show points to the next rank; trader pilots show lifetime earnings
    • Louder, rebalanced audio; weapon boosts last 1–3 minutes
  5. v8

    Rocks, harvesters and the full Codex

    • New giant-asteroid shapes, including rare ones with a tunnel bored right through
    • Internal scans take time, then map the veins from the surface inward
    • Salvage harvesters sweep the whole sector; a persistent, scrollable flight log
    • The Codex now documents every rule: drop chances, damage, prices, raids, missions
  6. v7

    Solid asteroids and docking

    • Big rocks are truly solid: each hit removes one voxel, and cut-loose pieces crumble
    • Giants up to 200 m drift around each sector; hidden ore, silicon, crystal and ice veins
    • Docking computers with real approach ranges; a laggy radar on stock kit
  7. v6

    Glitchy sensors and a tighter economy

    • Lock time and target data depend on distance; weak sensors glitch and garble
    • Global scans reveal hidden pact spies and deep-space signals
    • Armoured missiles, outpost grades I–V, flak point defence; money is harder to come by
  8. v5
